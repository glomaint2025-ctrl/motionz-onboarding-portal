Motionz Portal: go-live checklist

The portal runs on my test accounts today. To go live it needs accounts that Motionz owns, so you control your data, emails and files.

How it works. For items 2, 3 and 4 choose A (invite me and I set it up) or B (you do it and send me the result). The other items are short steps for you. Never send a password; A is always an invitation you can remove later. My email for invitations: __________________________. Your time: about 30–45 minutes in total.

Costs (approximate, check each provider): Vercel free to start, Pro about $20/month. Supabase free to start, Pro about $25/month (recommended for backups). Resend free up to 3,000 emails/month. Google Cloud (already set up) pay per use, small. Drive, GHL and Slack: nothing extra.

Good to know

  • Google only shares with Google accounts. Every staff email you add to the portal must be a Google account (Workspace, or a free Google account created on that email), or that person cannot open client folders and sheets. Today admin@motionz.ai is not one. Same for a client who wants to open their sheets or an uploaded contract.
  • Drive access follows the portal. Admins get every client folder, a CSM gets their own clients, a client gets their own files. Google emails each person a "shared with you" notice. Removing someone from the portal removes their Drive access.
  • The live portal starts empty. No test clients, leads or staff are carried over.

On go-live day, inside the portal (about 15 minutes)

  1. Change the admin password (the current one is a shared test password).
  2. Admin → Staff: add your real admins and CSMs (@motionz.ai emails). Set each CSM's booking calendar under Edit.
  3. Admin → Settings & Integrations → Notification emails: who gets onboarding forms, website requests and lead forms.
  4. Admin → Settings & Integrations → Staff sign-in security: emailed sign-in code on or off.
  5. Admin → Portal Templates and Video Scripts: check the wording new clients will see.
  6. Add your first real client, then paste their GHL Location ID in Admin → GHL Connect.
