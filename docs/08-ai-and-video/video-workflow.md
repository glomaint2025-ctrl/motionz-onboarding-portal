# Phase 8: Video Workflow and Production Preference

This document details the video workflow and client production preference management.

## 1. Production Preference Recording

The portal allows the client to record their production preference:

1. AI Video Preference:
   - Records client intent to utilize AI video workflows handled externally by Motionz.
   - Saves preference state in the client tenant record.
   - Note: Actual AI video generation and rendering is out of scope for this portal.

2. Self-Filmed Video Preference:
   - Records client intent to film video content themselves using the provided scripts.
   - Displays clear guidelines on orientation, lighting, and script delivery.

## 2. Client Experience

- The client visits the Video Scripts page in their portal.
- The client sees the 3 template scripts pre-filled with their contact and company name.
- The client toggles between AI Video and Self-Filmed Video preferences.
- The preference is saved to the database and visible to the assigned CSM.
