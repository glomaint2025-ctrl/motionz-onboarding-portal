# Phase 6: AI Assistant (Out of Scope)

## Status: OUT OF SCOPE

The AI Assistant module is explicitly excluded from the Motionz Onboarding Portal implementation scope.

### Scope Rulings
1. No conversational LLM chatbot is included in the client portal.
2. No natural language CRM execution (automated invoicing, messaging, contact creation) is supported.
3. No voice simulation or phone roleplay is supported.

The client portal navigation and routes do not contain an AI Assistant tab.
