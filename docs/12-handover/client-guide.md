# Motionz Client Portal User Manual & Guide

Welcome to the **Motionz Client Portal**. This guide assists roofing dealer owners and team members in accessing their private portal, tracking onboarding setup, managing leads, generating video scripts, and booking strategy calls.

---

## 1. Accessing Your Portal

1. **Invitation Link**: Check your email for your personal one-time invitation link:
   `https://portal.motionz.ai/auth/verify?token=...`
2. **Access Security**: Your link provides secure, passwordless authentication valid for 72 hours.
3. Upon clicking the link, your session is authenticated and you are automatically routed to your private company overview dashboard:
   `/portal/[your-company-slug]`

---

## 2. Navigating Core Portal Modules

The portal interface is designed for clean, rapid access on both desktop and mobile screens:

### 2.1. Overview (Dashboard)
- **Progress Metric**: Displays your live onboarding completion percentage.
- **Active Step**: Highlights the current milestone underway and what unlocks upon completion.
- **Quick Links**: Immediate access to your dedicated Slack channel, Skool training community, and onboarding forms.

### 2.2. Onboarding Setup Roadmap (`/onboarding`)
Displays the 5 key milestones to launch your campaigns:
1. **Google Sheet**: Intake details for territory and account parameters.
2. **GoHighLevel / A2P Verified**: CRM setup and phone texting compliance registration.
3. **Facebook**: Ad account and asset delegation.
4. **Domain, Email & Website**: Professional web and email infrastructure.
5. **Phone System & A2P Texting**: Live call forwarding and SMS outreach.

Each card displays:
- **Name & Owner**: Who is responsible.
- **Status**: Current state.
- **What it is**: Purpose of this milestone.
- **Right now**: Live real-time update from your Motionz account manager.
- **Unlocks**: Capabilities unlocked when finished.

### 2.3. Leads & Pipeline (`/leads`)
- View incoming homeowner inquiries, phone calls, and booked roof inspections.
- Filter by status: New, Contacted, Inspection Booked, Closed, Archive.
- Inspect lead name, contact info, property address, and source channel.

### 2.4. Campaign Tracking (`/tracking`)
- Live performance metrics synced from your active advertising campaigns.
- Track total impressions, clicks, lead volume, cost per lead (CPL), and return on ad spend.

### 2.5. Tools & Resources (`/tools`)
- **Video Scripts Generator**: Generate customized roof restoration and testimonial scripts with your company name, owner name, phone number, and state automatically inserted.
- **Video Preference**: Choose whether your marketing uses self-filmed authentic video or Motionz-produced footage.
- **Roof Measurement Calculator**: Calculate total roof squares, pitch multiplier, and chemical volume estimates.

### 2.6. Signed Contracts Vault (`/contract`)
- Review and download your signed Dealer Agreements and partner contracts securely.

### 2.7. Orders & Shipments (`/orders`)
- Track equipment, coatings, and marketing collateral shipments with real-time carrier tracking links.

### 2.8. Company Profile & Team (`/profile`, `/team`)
- Update business legal name, primary phone, address, and email.
- Invite team members with role-based access to the portal.

### 2.9. Book a Call (`/book-call`)
- Schedule a 1-on-1 strategy session directly with your CSM using the embedded calendar (Calendar ID `SRn2ONyB295xnnPR5JwR`).

## 3. Mobile & Desktop Experience

- **Desktop**: Full left navigation bar, expansive multi-column data views, and wide tables.
- **Mobile**: Responsive slide-out drawer menu, bottom quick-access navigation bar, and card-based touch-friendly layouts.
- Works in any modern mobile browser without requiring app store downloads.
